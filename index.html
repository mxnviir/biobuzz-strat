BIOBUZZ build call

Do we put a nectar placer on the robot, or leave it off and shoot faster? A flower pays a fixed 13 points if nobody can top your nectar, roughly half that if they can, and nothing if your partner already covers it. So the answer depends on how many other teams build one. Enter that share and the tool averages the gain over every alliance you could be drawn into.

What this assumes

  • Match is 30 s auto, 8 s transition, 120 s teleop (10.1). Nectar cannot enter a flower until the last 60 s (G410, major foul per nectar) and all remaining nectar unlocks at 60 s (G426), so every flower plan is a last-minute plan; only draining can happen earlier (G418).
  • Points from Table 10-2: tip 20, bottom nectar bonus 5, each element in an owned flower 2, park 5, leave 3. RP thresholds (Table 10-3): 4 and 7 tips for POLLINATOR, 16 leave+park points for SWARM.
  • Each flower starts with 4 pollen, but the bottom one sits below the middle ring and is outside the scoring volume (Figure 10-5), so an untouched flower holds 3 scoring pollen. One nectar on top makes it 5 + 2×4 = 13 points to that alliance.
  • Ownership goes to the top-most nectar, so the last placer wins; the bonus goes to the bottom-most, so the first placer wins. When both alliances can place, the model treats it as a race and gives us a share equal to our alliance's placement speed over the total. It does not model who runs out of nectar or clock first.
  • Opponents with a placer take every flower we leave alone, up to what their robots can reach in the window, and always try when they can. Draining 3 pollen out of a flower is legal for anyone with a floor-level intake and cuts what they can win there from 13 to 7. Drained pollen is not credited as fuel: after the first tip the floor is never the bottleneck.
  • Flower work is charged to the robot doing it, drive time included. While one robot is at a flower the other shoots alone; when both shoot, the crowding efficiency applies.
  • A ball is worth 20 ÷ balls-per-tip. Nectar spent on a flower is nectar not shot. The human player holds back the nectar for flowers in the alliance area until 1:00.
  • Garden points (1 each) and elements left in the cell (2 each) are the same for both builds and are left out.
  • Adoption tends to rise through the season. If your estimate sits near the break-even, plan for the higher number.
